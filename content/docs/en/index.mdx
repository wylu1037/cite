# Welcome to Cite

This is the English edition of the department knowledge base. Every answer has a source: browse the sidebar, search, or ask the assistant.

## Translation status

Pages are translated one by one. Until a page exists in English, it is **not** served here — the site does not fall back to the Chinese original, so what you read is always up to date.

Want a specific page in English? Add it under `content/docs/en/` with the same path as its Chinese counterpart (`content/docs/zh/...`).
